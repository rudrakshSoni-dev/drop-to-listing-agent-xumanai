- **Read these numbers as optimistic.** The extract_v5 prompt was revised after looking at extract_v4's failures on this same set: v4 → v5 took age accuracy from 85% to 92% and mode from 10/12 to 11/12. With no held-out set, some of that gain is fitting to these 26 videos.
- **The prompt revision also cost precision (57% → 47%).** v5 lists "expert" as a vague credential, so captions like "expert tutors in 50+ subjects" or "expert trainers" now raise an unverified-credential flag on five rows the labels call clean. Planned fix (v6): treat marketing adjectives as non-claims and keep the flag for specific-sounding credentials ("certified", "former teacher at X").
- **Long captions get cut.** Captions are trimmed to 1,000 characters, and the one remaining mode miss (Kod2Kids, "Offline & Online Program") sits past the cut. Planned fix (v6): keep more caption text, or the lines with contact and format details.
- **Local transcription is the weakest link.** faster-whisper small.en sometimes hallucinates on noisy, accented audio. In one art-teacher video the run's transcript lost the spoken website entirely, so the contact flag was missed (the only guardrail miss in both runs). Gemini transcription is more robust, but would double the free-tier requests per video.
- **The contact rules over-flag links.** Captions that only link to other YouTube videos, books or an app were flagged as off-platform contact (2 of the false positives). The rules can't tell a booking link from a playlist link; for a youth marketplace, holding for review is the right way to fail, but it costs precision.
- **The free tier makes runs mixed and slow.** Daily caps rotated this run across three models (12 rows on gemini-3.7-flash, 10 on gemini-3.1-flash-lite, 4 on gemini-3.8-flash), so accuracy blends models. The p95 latency comes from rows answered while gemini-3.7-flash was near its cap, plus the longest (2–3 minute) videos, where local transcription takes longest.
- **Categories are the easy part (26/26).** Titles and captions usually name the subject. With 1–3 rows for most categories, this says little about hard boundaries such as homework help versus tutoring.
